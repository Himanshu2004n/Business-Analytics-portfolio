# Global AI Supply Chain & Geopolitical Risk Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is a multi-page Business Intelligence dashboard built using SQL and Power BI to analyze the global AI supply chain ecosystem, geopolitical risks, semiconductor trade dynamics, infrastructure investments, and macroeconomic performance.

The dashboard combines multiple datasets to generate strategic insights related to:

* Global AI infrastructure investments
* Semiconductor trade flows
* Geopolitical sanctions and restrictions
* Economic growth and AI competitiveness
* Energy infrastructure readiness
* Strategic technology leadership

The project demonstrates SQL querying, relational data modeling, KPI development, dashboard storytelling, and executive-level business analytics.

# Dashboard Pages

## Page 1: Global AI Supply Chain Overview

### Key Insights

* Global AI investments accelerated significantly after 2021.
* Semiconductor trade remains concentrated among a few major economies.
* Advanced hardware dominates semiconductor trade value.
* AI infrastructure investments continue increasing globally.

### Visuals Used

* KPI Cards
* Line Chart
* Donut Chart
* Interactive Slicers

---

## Page 2: Geopolitical Risk Analytics

### Key Insights

* China, Taiwan, Russia, and India show high geopolitical exposure.
* Export bans and tariffs are the most common trade restrictions.
* Technology sanctions significantly impact semiconductor ecosystems.

### Visuals Used

* KPI Cards
* Bar Charts
* Treemap
* Geopolitical Risk Map

## Page 3: Infrastructure & Economic Intelligence
