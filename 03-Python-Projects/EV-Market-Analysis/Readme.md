# 🚗 EV Market Analysis using Python

## 📊 Overview
This project analyzes Electric Vehicle (EV) data to understand key trends in pricing, range, performance, and market positioning.

The objective is to convert raw data into meaningful insights that support business-level decision making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze EV pricing distribution and segmentation  
- Understand the relationship between range and price  
- Identify dominant brands and market segments  
- Evaluate performance metrics across vehicles  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Python  
- Pandas  
- NumPy  
- Matplotlib  

---

## 📁 Dataset
The dataset contains EV specifications including:
- Price (Euro)  
- Range (km)  
- Efficiency (Wh/km)  
- Acceleration and Top Speed  
- Brand, Segment, and Powertrain  

---

## 📈 Key Analysis

### 💰 Price Distribution
- Wide variation in EV prices  
- Clear segmentation between budget and premium vehicles  

### ⚡ Range Analysis
- Significant variation across models  
- Range is a key decision factor  

### 🔗 Price vs Range
- Strong positive relationship  
- Higher range vehicles tend to have higher prices  

### 🚗 Brand & Segment Analysis
- Few brands dominate the market  
- EVs are concentrated in specific segments  

### 🔥 Correlation Analysis
- Strong relationship between price and range  
- Moderate link between performance variables  

---

## 🧠 Key Insights
- EV market is highly segmented by price  
- Range is the strongest driver of pricing  
- Premium brands dominate higher segments  
- Performance metrics are relatively consistent  
- Value for money varies across models  

---

## 💼 Business Implications
- Improving range can increase competitiveness  
- Strategic pricing is essential for market positioning  
- Brand strength directly impacts pricing power  

---

## 🏁 Conclusion
The EV market is driven by range, pricing, and brand positioning.  
Companies focusing on range optimization and cost efficiency will gain a competitive advantage.
